App certificate & key

If the Leapmotor app certificate or private key changes, you can update it here without removing or reinstalling the app. Press Download to fetch the current files (the download links are read from the link file in the app's repository), or paste them manually and press Save. The new certificate and key are applied immediately. Leave the fields untouched to keep the current values.
